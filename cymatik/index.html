The Core Experience

See your music.

CYMATIK turns the movement and energy inside audio into a responsive visual space. The result is not a passive video—it is an environment shaped by sound as it happens.

Sound becomes terrain

Live waveform history rises into a dimensional landscape, translating musical motion into depth, color, and light.

Shape the world

Controls for terrain, color, lighting, display, presets, and output let each performance take on its own visual character.

Built for the moment

Designed around real-time operation so the visual environment can follow a track, a set, or an evolving creative session.

Creative Control

From signal to scene.

The CYMATIK control panel keeps the musical source, visual response, and output tools in one focused workspace.

Live waveform

Shape the amplitude, thickness, brightness, gravity, fade, and blending of the central signal.

Terrain

Adjust sensitivity, relief, proximity, expansion, speed influence, memory drift, and landscape lift.

Colour & light

Control intensity, peak emission, bloom, contour strength, and summit whitening.

Presets and output

Move between visual directions, save and restore settings, and capture the CYMATIK canvas.
